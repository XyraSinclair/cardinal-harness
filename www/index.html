Sorting with language models, measured

Sorting a list well is where judgement becomes action.

Every allocation, priority queue, grant screen, moderation decision, and “which matters more” is a sort. Language models can sort — sometimes, for some attributes, under some presentations. This is the working hub for making when into a number people can read, making that number cheaper to obtain, and trying every trick in the book with the evidence attached.

llmsorting.com hosts the methods, the robustness metrics, and the experiments. The engine, the ledger, and the benchmark live on the sister sites below.

One instrument, four faces

The stack turns noisy pairwise ratio judgements — “how many times more of X does A have than B?” — into cardinal scores with uncertainty, and tests whether those judgements deserve the name belief: a belief is a fixed point of the transformations that shouldn’t matter.

The grok gauge

A judge groks an attribute over an entity pool when its judgements behave like noisy readings of one latent scalar. Grokking is a property of the triple (model, attribute wording, entity pool) — never of a model alone. The root claim, in log space:

m(A,B) = s(A) − s(B)

The gauge is a small set of falsifiable readouts. Each has a denominator and a null — what a judge that is not reading a latent shows. Every readout already exists as an engine diagnostic; the program’s job is a fixed protocol, a public presentation, and calibration against ground truth.

Order invarianceSame pair, slots swapped: direction agreement and mean |Δ ln r|. Null: 50% agreement — a coin flip.
Reciprocity“How many times more” vs “how many times less”: ln r₊ + ln r₋ ≈ 0. Null: drift far from zero.
Direction transitivityOver triads with repeat draws: weak / moderate / strong stochastic transitivity violations deeper than 2 SE. Null: majority cycles beyond sampling noise.
Multiplicative closureAround triangles: ln r_ab + ln r_bc + ln r_ca ≈ 0 — the cyclic residual (Hodge curl) fraction. Null: curl mass far above the noise floor.
PolarityNegate the attribute: correlation of latents ≈ −1. Null: ≈ 0 (attribute ignored) or positive.
ParaphraseReword the attribute: rank correlation ≈ +1. Null: low.
SignalSpread of latents beyond posterior noise. Null: flat — nothing was measured.
Null calibrationAn irrelevant attribute produces no phantom structure. Null: structure appears anyway.

Composite, as on the benchmark: signal × mean coherence — a constant judge scores 0, a decisive incoherent judge scores low. For people, three bands, stated as conventions until calibrated:

Grokkedorder agreement ≥ 0.9 · no WST violation beyond 2 SE · curl fraction ≤ 0.1 · polarity ≤ −0.8 · paraphrase ≥ 0.8
Partialsignal present; one or more readouts fail their threshold
Notat null on order or transitivity, or no signal

The bands are earned, not decreed. Experiment E2 runs the gauge on anchor pools with known true ratios — countries by population, rivers by length — where grokking is externally checkable, and adjusts the thresholds until the gauge agrees with truth there. Only then is it read on attributes without ground truth. Every band shown will carry its denominators (pairs, triads, swapped pairs, runs) beside it.

The experiment ladder

Each rung produces one evidence pack — report.json, trace.jsonl, a RESULTS with denominators — and one page here. Status is one of planned in flight executed; executed means an observed after-state, never a plan.

  1. E1 — Setwise ratio with a cached entity prefix.executed Three or four entities in the prompt prefix, the attribute last; k−1 log-ratios per call lowered to pairwise evidence; counterbalanced by pivot rotation; solved and compared against the canonical pairwise sort on the same items. Run 2026-08-15, gpt-4.1-mini, 372/372 calls parsed, $0.209 total. Caching works: after the first attribute per entity block, 75–78% of prompt tokens read from cache; 5,370 pairwise-equivalent observations per dollar vs 2,049 for the pairwise baseline (2.6×). And the instrument found a pathology: a pivot halo — the model rated 795/870 ratios below the reference slot (mean −0.95 nats), and rotating the pivot flipped 80–93% of implied pair directions, versus 6–38% positional flips on the pairwise path. Agreement with pairwise latents ρ 0.29–0.81 by attribute. Cheap observations are worthless if the reference frame owns the answer — exactly what the gauge exists to catch. Evidence pack →
  2. E2 — Grok gauge calibration on anchors.executed Three anchor pools with known true ratios (countries by population, rivers by length, cities by metro population) × two judges (gpt-4.1-mini, gpt-5.4-nano); 768/768 calls parsed, $0.107. The gauge separates models — mini scored partial on every pool, nano scored not on every pool, matching truth-rank order everywhere. And the run exposed two honest defects: the composite band loses what its components see (truth ρ 0.965 and 0.350 both landed "partial"; curl 0.039 vs 0.129 told them apart), and no cell reached "grokked" — the provisional order ≥ 0.90 and polarity ≤ −0.80 gates are too strict at a 64-comparison budget. Evidence-backed proposal recorded: gate on curl ≤ 0.10 AND order ≥ 0.70 (separates truth-good from truth-bad 6/6 here), demote polarity to a diagnostic. Bonus finding: judges compress true log-ratios to ~⅓ of their real magnitude wherever rank is good. Evidence pack →
  3. E3 — Attribute-swap economics.planned Many attributes over one fixed entity block: measured cost per attribute as the count grows, versus pairwise per attribute.
  4. E4 — Comparator-sort zoo.planned Merge sort, quicksort, tournament, and the active planner over an LLM comparator with measured noise: comparisons to a certified top-k, error under intransitivity.
  5. E5 — Listwise vs pairwise vs setwise at n = 8, 16, 32, 64.planned Where each breaks, on the same pool and attribute.
  6. E6 — Best–worst scaling.planned The highest-value missing cell in the instrument grid.
  7. E7 — PMF evidence per instrument.planned Where providers expose logprobs: separation per dollar versus point answers.

Why this matters

LLM-as-judge is production infrastructure now: reward models, eval pipelines, ranking, moderation, grant screening. None of it works if the judge contradicts itself under a permutation it should not notice. Agreement with a reference tells you nothing about that; only the judge’s behaviour under transformation does. Sorting is the smallest task where the failure is visible, the cheapest to measure, and the one every larger decision reduces to.
