FEYNMAN SEAT — REALITY CONTACT REPORT

1. CORE JUDGMENT

The Manifund P2 professed-vs-revealed result is the one real finding in the portfolio — with one soft leg and one confound the headline papers over. I verified it down to the JSON: the fitted weights (epistemic_integrity 0.794, impact_per_dollar 0.000, artifacts/live/manifund-p2-2026-07-19/acx2024-explain.json) are not a fit artifact, because the univariate Spearmans independently agree (0.360 vs 0.049) — the inversion does not rest on unstable multivariate weights over collinear regressors. Pre-registration is genuine (predictions committed before unblinding; one honestly-quoted join fix that changed no statistic). Mechanism, step by step: (a) P1 canonized four attribute wordings for cross-judge transmissibility; (b) each of 83 ACX proposals got cardinal scores per attribute via counterbalanced pairwise ratio judgments (deepseek judge, IRLS solve); (c) the combined score predicted the funded set at AUC 0.635, registered blind; (d) `explain` fit the four attribute-score vectors against the funded-only dollar order — EV/$ gets zero weight, epistemic integrity dominates; (e) the EA Community Choice replication (crowd quadratic matching) is stronger overall AND flips the profile: impact_per_dollar becomes the best single predictor (AUC 0.682 vs 0.557 juried).

BUT the "professed" leg is not what the headline implies. I traced it to P1's `weigh`: an AHP over MODEL-PROPOSED considerations, judged BY AN LLM, under the goal prompt "allocate Manifund's marginal $10,000 grant for maximal expected impact." EV/$ dominating "professed" weight is partly baked into the elicitation's own framing. This is an LLM's rendering of community discourse, not the community professing anything. The revealed leg (actual funding) is real ground truth; the professed leg is soft. The defensible headline is the ACX↔EACC attribute-profile flip, which needs no professed leg at all — and that flip has its own concealed confound: the two mechanisms were measured on two different proposal cohorts. Mechanism and cohort are perfectly confounded; "the gap is a property of the funding mechanism" is currently under-identified.

The axis-research wave 2 is superb instrument engineering presented at the edge of overclaim. I verified the scar_tissue_density inversion in the raw sort JSONs: the confabulated war story (item-0001: "3 AM, 10,000 records, 48 hours straight") ranks 10/9 for opus/sol and 1st for gpt-5.4-mini, while both frontiers top-rank the mechanism-bearing mundane items ($6,140 tokenizer bill, 97 MB chunk, 573° quartz soak) — exactly as claimed, with 0/12 position flips on both frontier runs. The plausible mechanism is real and interesting: judging "specificity only contact with the territory produces" requires a prior over what genuine operational detail looks like versus what plausible extrapolation generates, and that prior scales with model quality; the mini falls back on narrative salience. But as a FINDING ("cheap models provably cannot see scar tissue") it rests on: one small model standing in for an entire tier; 12 items; one run per cell; no test-retest baseline (the tier gaps of rho 0.2–0.4 have no within-model run-noise denominator); and — deepest — items and decoys authored by a frontier-family agent, so "what frontiers recognize" may partly be "what the author's peer models recognize." Wave 2 is currently a demonstration that one specific mini misranks one planted item per axis. The honest WEAK verdicts and the "the wording IS the axis" lesson are genuinely valuable; the tier-law framing is not yet earned.

Rigor×ambition "orthogonality" (+0.072, CI spanning zero) is a null measurement on one corpus with one judge pair — calibration, not a finding. Judge portfolio theory and the logprob census are instrument economics — real, useful, not profound. JCB is a product with an external consumer, not a result.

2. STRONGEST OBJECTION TO THE CURRENT TRAJECTORY

The program generates new instruments and probe waves faster than it converts any single result into something that survives hostile scrutiny. P2's headline is one second-judge replication (~$0.35, explicitly costed in the notes themselves) and one deconfound away from being either undeniable or dead — and instead of buying those two cheap tests, effort went to an 8-axis wave 2 with self-authored items. The repo's own red-team said it: the validation loop closes through one person. Every result I checked is INTERNALLY honest (denominators quoted, failures reported) and EXTERNALLY untested — no second judge on the ACX fit, no second small model on the axis probes, no cohort-controlled mechanism comparison. Reality contact means letting something outside the authoring loop get a vote.

3. MOVES (≤3)

MOVE 1 — Deconfound mechanism from cohort on the same slate (the decisive test for the most profound result). Manifund live projects receive both regrantor decisions and retail crowd donations. Take one cohort where both dollar streams exist (the already-planned P3 live slate, or EA CC if its transaction data splits by donor class — ground_truth.csv has n_txns but not the split; the Manifund API scrape is free), score attributes once, then fit revealed weights separately against regrantor-dollars and crowd-dollars ON THE SAME PROPOSALS. If the epistemic-integrity↔EV/$ flip reproduces within a single cohort, the mechanism claim becomes undeniable; if it vanishes, it was cohort composition. Marginal elicitation cost ≈ $0–1 given P3 is planned anyway. Loser condition: retail-donation volume on the juried slate too thin (a few txns per project) to define a crowd order — check that denominator before running.

MOVE 2 — Buy the two cheap kills for P2: second judge + polish control. Re-run the ACX `explain` fit with one frontier judge (cache makes it ~$0.35 by the pack's own accounting), and add one control attribute ("overall writing quality/polish") to test whether epistemic_integrity carries weight beyond a general well-written-proposal factor — the strongest mundane alternative ("juried readers reward careful prose, crowds skim") is currently unexcluded. Loser condition: if the second judge's position-flip rate matches deepseek's 25%, add a third rather than adjudicating two noisy votes — and if that spirals past ~$2, stop and report the ambiguity.

MOVE 3 — Convert wave 2 from demo to finding: replicate the frozen probe sets across the small tier + measure run noise. Same frozen item files, 2–3 additional small models (haiku-class, flash-class, qwen-class) and one repeat run per existing cell for a test-retest rho baseline. ~$2–4 at probe pricing. If the scar_tissue inversion holds across small models and the tier gaps exceed the measured run-noise floor, "tier-divergent axes" becomes publishable; if it's gpt-5.4-mini-specific, wave 2 gets honestly relabeled as decoy-instrument validation. Do NOT reword any axis before this runs — the frozen wordings are the instrument. Loser condition: if within-model test-retest rho at 24 comparisons comes back below ~0.8, the probe size itself is under-powered and the fix is more comparisons per cell, not more models.
