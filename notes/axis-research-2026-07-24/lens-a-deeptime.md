# Lens A generator output — deep time / eschatology / the sacred (2026-07-24)

## sub_specie_aeternitatis
- **def**: A has more than B when A views its subject from eternity's vantage — its weighings would still register as weighings to a mind standing outside A's century — while B's frame silently assumes the present moment matters because it is present.
- **smell**: Requires simulating a reader decoupled from the author's era and checking which of the text's load-bearing valuations survive the decoupling; that counterfactual reader is dense latent structure, not a surface feature.
- **top**: A metaphysical treatise in the Spinoza register — addressed to no particular century, weighing human affairs as a geometer weighs figures.
- **confounder**: Archaic or formal diction and the absence of dates.

## decade_escape_velocity
- **def**: A > B when A's argument survives transplantation three centuries forward or backward with less loss — fewer of its load-bearing premises are secretly its own decade's furniture.
- **smell**: Judging this means identifying which premises are era-local contingencies of intellectual history, which requires actually knowing that history, not detecting topical keywords.
- **top**: An essay from the 1750s (or 1990s) that reads as if written yesterday and will read that way in 2300.
- **confounder**: Mere absence of topical references, dates, and proper nouns.

## cathedral_time_horizon
- **def**: A > B when the project A serves completes further beyond its author's death — oak-beam planting treated as ordinary work rather than announced as vision.
- **smell**: The payoff schedule of an ambition must be inferred from structure and temperament, not stated dates; distinguishing genuine multi-generation commitment from futurist costume is exactly what collapses in small models.
- **top**: An engineering or institutional proposal whose success criterion sits 200+ years out and whose author writes about it in the tone of a Tuesday.
- **confounder**: Explicit "long-term"/"future generations" vocabulary and big year numbers.

## unborn_addressivity
- **def**: A > B when A is more genuinely composed for readers who do not yet exist — it anticipates their ignorance of our context and provides for it inside the text.
- **smell**: Requires modeling the knowledge state of a reader with no shared context and checking whether the text actually funds that reader, versus apostrophizing "posterity" while leaning on 2020s common ground.
- **top**: The Sandia nuclear-waste marker report register — "this place is not a place of honor" — engineering a message across a 10,000-year semantic gap.
- **confounder**: Rhetorical address to "future generations" as flourish.

## eschatological_seriousness
- **def**: A > B when A treats ultimate outcomes — extinction, the closing of the future, final accounting — as literal, action-guiding facts rather than mood, genre, or brand.
- **smell**: Live belief and its aesthetic performance can be sentence-for-sentence identical; separating them is deep pragmatics — what the text does downstream of the claim, what it reallocates.
- **top**: A quiet memo in which someone reorders their life's work because an argument about the far future went through.
- **confounder**: X-risk/apocalypse keyword density and doomy affect.

## one_way_door_gravity
- **def**: A > B when A more fully reckons with irreversibility — losses and choices no future agent can undo — and lets that asymmetry restructure its reasoning rather than merely darken its tone.
- **smell**: Requires tracking whether non-undoability actually does inferential work (changes the decision calculus, the ethics, the tempo) versus appearing as warning-shaped decoration.
- **top**: An essay on species extinction or first-contact protocol that derives its entire ethics from the one-way property itself.
- **confounder**: Doom sentiment and urgency vocabulary.

## copernican_vertigo
- **def**: A > B when A makes the actual scale of space and time *felt* and lets that felt scale re-weigh human concerns, rather than citing large numbers as ornament.
- **smell**: Whether scale is metabolized or decorative shows up only in the downstream consequences within the text — a model must trace what the vastness changes, which is invisible to lexical scoring.
- **top**: The Pale Blue Dot passage register — cosmic scale turned into a moral instrument in a single movement.
- **confounder**: Astronomy vocabulary and big-number counts.

## kalpa_patience
- **def**: A > B when A's temporal temperament embodies more capacity to wait at civilizational or cosmic timescale — patience that is a form of power, not resignation.
- **smell**: Patience and passivity are lexically near-identical; telling equanimity-with-agency from quietism requires modeling the author's live relationship to time.
- **top**: A monastic rule or Long Now-style institutional design written to run unattended for centuries and visibly at peace with that.
- **confounder**: Calm, slow, meditative tone.

## mysterium_tremendum
- **def**: A > B when A induces more of the numinous double-charge — dread and attraction before something wholly other — in a competent reader.
- **smell**: The numinous is a reader-state produced by restraint, pacing, and ontological positioning; small models score church words, and the strongest instances often contain none.
- **top**: The voice-from-the-whirlwind register of Job 38–41; certain Dickinson quatrains.
- **confounder**: Density of sacred/religious vocabulary.

## liturgical_density

- **top**: A Book of Common Prayer collect; the Meditations — texts whose native use is recitation as practice.
- **confounder**: Surface repetition, anaphora, refrain structure.

## votive_costliness

- **smell**: Detecting paid cost versus performed confession requires a model of what was expensive to say, where, and when — a counterfactual over the author's world, not a sentiment read.
- **top**: A work written in full knowledge it could not be published in its author's lifetime — the Life and Fate register.
- **confounder**: First-person suffering narration and confessional tone.

## covenant_weight

- **confounder**: Legalistic or oath-like phrasing.

## sole_witness_weight

- **top**: The last speaker's field notes on a now-extinct language; testimony of an otherwise undocumented atrocity.
- **confounder**: Topical obscurity.

## oblivion_defiance

- **top**: Requiem; the memorial books of destroyed communities, written so that erasure would not be total.
- **confounder**: Elegiac or nostalgic tone.

## palimpsest_depth

- **confounder**: Quotation and allusion counts.

## rosetta_load

## ruin_value

- **top**: Heraclitus, Sappho: works we in fact possess only as fragments, and which still burn.
- **confounder**: Aphoristic style and tweetable lines.

## memento_mori_depth

- **confounder**: Death-word frequency and morbid subject matter.
