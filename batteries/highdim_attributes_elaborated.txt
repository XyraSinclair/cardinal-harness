self-containedness: how completely the text stands on its own — a reader needs no outside context, links, or prior knowledge to fully grasp it. Reward closure and sufficiency; do not reward mere brevity.
high-status: how much the text signals membership in an elite or prestigious in-group — credentials, powerful affiliations, insider confidence. Reward markers of social rank; do not reward the underlying quality of the work.
low-status: how much the text signals marginality or lack of social standing — hedging, outsider framing, apology, scrappiness. Reward markers of low rank; do not reward genuine humility of a secure author.
poshness: how upper-class, refined, or genteel the register feels — polished diction, understatement, cultivated taste. Reward refinement of manner; do not reward wealth of subject matter alone.
technical calmness: how composed and unhurried the technical voice is — precise, load-bearing claims stated without hype or anxiety. Reward serene mastery; do not reward simplicity or lack of ambition.
earnestness: how sincerely and un-ironically the text means what it says — direct conviction, absence of hedging distance or knowing winks. Reward wholehearted sincerity; do not reward naivety.
intellectual density: how many distinct load-bearing ideas per sentence — compression of real content, not padding. Reward idea-per-word richness; do not reward jargon or long words.
legibility to an outsider: how easily a smart reader from outside the field grasps the point. Reward clarity across the boundary; do not reward dumbing-down that loses the substance.
coiled potential energy: how much latent, not-yet-released force the text implies — a sense that something big could unfold from it. Reward pent tension and upside; do not reward mere present size.
craftedness: how visibly the text was shaped with care — deliberate structure, chosen words, finished edges. Reward evident craft; do not reward ornament for its own sake.
institutional insiderness: how much the author writes from inside an established institution's norms and machinery. Reward fluency in the institutional idiom; do not reward the institution's prestige itself.
how much it rewards a careful re-read: how much additional meaning a second, slower reading yields. Reward layered depth; do not reward obscurity that hides nothing.
