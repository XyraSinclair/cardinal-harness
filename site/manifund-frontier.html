Manifund · cost–validity frontier

How much of the judgment do cheaper methods recover?

The Manifund page ranks proposals by thousands of pairwise LLM judgments. This companion eval asks what lexical features and frozen-embedding probes recover of that signal, per attribute — and how much the judges agree with each other in the first place. Forty proposals, one-line entities, rank statistics; directional, not decisive.

Findings (2026-08-16, n=40 — directional)

  • Ceiling. Inter-judge Spearman on the 32-battery: median 0.67 (gemma31b–gemma26b 0.70, gemma31b–gemini 0.69). Best-agreed: empirical testability 0.81, technical depth 0.79, downside risk 0.78. Worst: robustness to assumptions 0.35, counterfactual impact 0.45. The 1,001 subtle attributes replicate within-model at median 0.46 (IQR 0.35–0.56) at the 240-comparison budget — half the battery's reliability; a campaign finding, not a method one.
  • Structure, not halo. PC1 explains 31% (32-battery), 22% (subtle). PC1 loads + on existential seriousness, long-run impact, ambition, downside risk, AI-timeline sensitivity and − on tractability, feasibility, maturity, robustness: an ambitious-speculative vs tractable-grounded axis. Judges are not collapsing to one quality factor.
  • Embedding probes (LOO ridge, 40 items) reach 0.5–0.6 on epistemic rigor (0.61), speed to first result (0.62), technical depth (0.53), testability (0.51) — roughly 75–85% of the inter-judge ceiling on those; near zero on neglectedness, fundability, counterfactual impact. embeddinggemma on the one-liner is the most frequent best variant; descriptions did not reliably help.
  • Lexical single features top out ~0.5 and the winner is usually word count; multi-feature ridge is weak (≤0.48). Line-length correlates 0.5–0.56 with gold on quantitative reasoning, concreteness of milestones, clarity of ToC, information value — flagged. Longer blurbs may genuinely carry more concrete content, or judges reward length; the full-description phase decides.
  • Cost. ~12 pairwise LLM calls per entity-attribute vs one local embedding forward pass vs free — the frontier is steep, and the probe arm's validity is real on the epistemic-content attributes.

Open: rerun when the 1,269-pool phases land (pull_gold.py); add an absolute-scoring cheap-LLM rung; test whether the probe generalizes across pools.
