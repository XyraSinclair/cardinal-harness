# Lens D generator output — demand-side: who pays to sort by this (2026-07-24)

## 1. live_wire_prose
- **def:** A has more live-wire than B when its texture reads as produced mid-act by someone bearing real obstacles — obsession-shaped specificity, contact with named difficulties, evidence of iterations already survived — rather than by someone describing intentions.
- **buyer:** Seed investors and scout programs triaging cold inbound and pre-network founders; feeds the take-the-meeting decision, currently ~$500/hr of partner reading time.
- **smell:** Requires inferring the generative process behind the text — was this mind actually in the arena — which means simulating what arena-contact does to prose, not matching its surface.
- **confounder:** Hustle-register density: confidence vocabulary, "we're crushing it" cadence, startup-speak.

## 2. orphaned_importance
- **def:** A has more than B when the problem it addresses matters more relative to the total serious attention the world currently gives it, and the text treats it with commensurate gravity rather than as a hook.
- **buyer:** Grantmakers and science philanthropies (EA funds, FROs, ARIA-style programs) allocating across problem areas; feeds portfolio-level field selection.
- **smell:** The ratio needs both a world-model of what matters and a map of the global attention landscape — the denominator is invisible to any model that only sees the text.
- **confounder:** Obscurity itself: a small model scores "niche and earnest" as "neglected and important."

## 3. counterfactual_hinge
- **def:** A hinges more than B when the described work is less likely to exist at all absent this specific actor and this funding — fewer substitute teams, less field momentum already pointing there.
- **buyer:** Grantmakers optimizing counterfactual impact at the individual-grant level; feeds fund/decline on specific proposals.
- **smell:** Requires modeling the field's default trajectory — who else is positioned to do this, and would they — which is a simulation of the world without the applicant.
- **confounder:** Self-described uniqueness; every proposal claims no one else can do it.

## 4. taste_under_constraint
- **def:** A shows more than B when its design choices reveal an internalized cost model — visible restraint, the right things deliberately not built, boring primitives where boring wins — versus choices that merely accumulated.
- **buyer:** Recruiters and hiring managers sourcing senior engineers from public repos and design docs; feeds the reach-out list, currently built by expensive engineer-hours of repo reading.
- **smell:** Judging what is absent requires generating the alternative designs the author rejected and pricing them; a shallow model can only see what is present.
- **confounder:** Style-guide conformance and cleanliness — lint-passing tidiness read as taste.

## 5. trajectory_steepness
- **def:** A is steeper than B when the difficulty-adjusted gap between their earliest and latest public work implies faster compounding of capability, holding time elapsed comparable.
- **buyer:** Talent programs (Emergent Ventures, Thiel Fellowship), junior-hiring pipelines, and seed investors betting on people before results; feeds bet-on-the-person decisions.
- **smell:** Needs difficulty adjustment across domains and eras — was the later work actually harder, or just more polished — which requires knowing what was hard when.
- **confounder:** Recency polish: newer work looks better because conventions moved, not because the mind did.

## 6. preconsensus_prescience
- **def:** A is more prescient than B when it committed, earlier and against thicker consensus, to specific claims that later resolved true — weighted by how contrarian the claim was at its timestamp.
- **buyer:** Forecasting shops and funds building analyst rosters, media hiring columnists, agent builders weighting sources by track record; feeds whose-feed-to-trust.
- **smell:** Requires reconstructing the historical belief landscape at each claim's date — what did informed people believe in March 2019 — which lives only in a model with deep, dateable world-knowledge.
- **confounder:** Retroactive vagueness: Nostradamus-shaped prose that fits any outcome, plus sheer confidence.

## 7. earned_confidence
- **def:** A has more than B when the strength of its stated confidence tracks eventual resolution across its claims — bold where right, hedged where wrong — rather than uniform in either direction.
- **buyer:** Forecasting platforms recruiting forecasters, funds weighting internal analysts, editors deciding whose predictions to platform; complements preconsensus_prescience with the confidence-accuracy coupling.
- **smell:** Requires extracting implicit probability from prose register and settling each claim against world knowledge; the coupling is invisible without both.
- **confounder:** Hedging density — a small model reads pervasive qualifiers as calibration.

## 8. draft_voltage
- **def:** A carries more voltage than B when a serious reader finishes it changed — some prior actually reordered — versus finishing informed but rearranged in no way.
- **buyer:** Editors at magazines and publishing houses triaging slush and commissioning; feeds accept/reject/develop, currently the scarcest editorial instinct on staff.
- **smell:** Must simulate a reader's belief-state before and after and diff them; surface affect and topic heat are exactly what this is not.
- **confounder:** Emotional intensity and hot-take framing.

## 9. authorial_irreducibility
- **def:** A is more irreducible than B when its texture could not be produced by a competent imitator or a model without the underlying experiences and mind — the prose is a costly signal of its own provenance.
- **buyer:** Editors, admissions offices, and investors reading founder memos in the LLM era; feeds is-there-a-mind-here triage, a brand-new expense line since 2023.
- **smell:** This is generative-process attribution: the judge must ask what it would take to fake this, which requires being the strongest available faker.
- **confounder:** Stylistic quirkiness — voice-shaped tics that are themselves the easiest thing to imitate.

## 10. unflinchingness
- **def:** A is more unflinching than B when it volunteers specific facts against the author's own interest — the damning number, the failed experiment, the churned customer — where B manages the reader.
- **buyer:** Investors doing diligence on founder updates, grantmakers reading progress reports, LPs reading fund letters; feeds continue/double-down/walk decisions.
- **smell:** The judge must model what this author, in this position, would prefer to hide — then check whether it surfaced. That counterfactual is position-specific and deep.
- **confounder:** Performative humility: "lessons learned" sections and ritual self-deprecation.

## 11. keystone_load
- **def:** A bears more load than B when removing it from a curriculum collapses more downstream understanding — more later ideas silently presuppose it.
- **buyer:** Curriculum builders, bootcamps, corporate L&D, and agent builders curating finite context windows; feeds what-makes-the-reading-list and what-earns-context-tokens.
- **smell:** Requires the dependency graph of ideas — which understanding presupposes which — a structure only a model that has itself learned the field holds.
- **confounder:** Citation count and canonical reputation.

## 12. epistemic_potability
- **def:** A is more potable than B when an agent ingesting it uncritically ends up with fewer false or subtly miscalibrated beliefs — safe to drink without boiling.
- **buyer:** Agent and RAG builders choosing corpus sources and per-domain trust weights; feeds ingest/exclude/discount decisions now made by vibes and domain lists.
- **smell:** Requires checking claims against a world model, and specifically detecting confident subtle wrongness — the failure mode that authoritative tone is optimized to hide.
- **confounder:** Institutional reputation and authoritative register.

## 13. idea_fertility
- **def:** A is more fertile than B when it opens more tractable follow-on problems than it closes — reading it generates a research program rather than a conclusion.
- **buyer:** Research funders and program managers (FROs, ARIA, private science) selecting programs; deep-tech scouts choosing which labs to court.
- **smell:** Counting downstream ideas requires generating them — the judge must actually do a step of the follow-on work in latent space to know it exists.
- **confounder:** Open-question lists and speculation volume; asking questions is not the same as opening tractable ones.

## 14. ripe_problem_nose
- **def:** A's body of work shows more nose than B's when its chosen problems sat closer to important-and-just-now-tractable at the moment they were chosen — Hamming's question, answered repeatedly in the affirmative.
- **buyer:** Lab directors and research recruiters hiring PIs and senior scientists; feeds who-gets-the-lab decisions currently made by committee folklore.
- **smell:** Ripeness-at-time-of-choice requires reconstructing the tractability frontier as it stood then, not as hindsight flattened it.
- **confounder:** Working on currently-hot topics — heat now read backward as nose then.

## 15. implementation_grip
- **def:** A grips more than B when its proposals survive contact with the named machinery — budget lines, statutory hooks, veto players, procurement reality — rather than terminating at "policymakers should."
- **buyer:** Think tanks, policy philanthropies, and legislative staff triaging white papers; feeds which-proposal-gets-the-champion.
- **smell:** Requires knowing how institutions actually move — the informal veto points and implementation pathways that are documented nowhere the text can crib from.
- **confounder:** Wonk vocabulary and acronym density.

## 16. heterodoxy_with_brakes
- **def:** A has more than B when its departures from consensus keep the error-correction machinery intact — strongest counterarguments engaged, falsifiers stated, exit conditions named — versus heterodoxy that has cut the brake lines.
- **buyer:** Heterodox-science funders, contrarian-essay editors, and fringe-tech investors, all of whom currently pay expensive human judgement to separate pre-paradigm genius from crankery.
- **smell:** Crank-vs-visionary is precisely where cheap heuristics fail symmetrically; the judge must engage the actual arguments deeply enough to tell a live disagreement from a broken one.
- **confounder:** Two-sided: consensus-conformity scored as sanity, or contrarian style scored as vision.

**Killed in generation:** decade-survival / outlives-its-news-peg (ceded to the deeptime lens), moat-legibility and distribution-instinct (reduce to business-quality slop), rereadability (reduces to quality), sentence-necessity (editors' version of clarity slop).
