# Lens B generator output — wattage / agency / live-wire (2026-07-24)

Culled from ~24 drafts by the generator. Several live directly on the
real-vs-performed-voltage line (voltage_under_load, voltage_off_camera,
live_computation, stake_density, refusal_to_stay_dead, annealed_conviction,
recovery_torque).

## voltage_under_load
- **def:** A has more than B when A's intensity *rises* at the genuinely hardest point of its own material, while B's intensity peaks in the opening and decays into gesture exactly where the difficulty starts.
- **smell:** The judge must itself locate where the hard part of the content lives — which requires being able to do the hard part — and then measure engagement gradient across it; a small model can't find the load point, only the enthusiasm.
- **top:** A systems or proof writeup where the author visibly accelerates into the section everyone else hand-waves.
- **confounder:** Enthusiasm-word density and exclamation cadence.

## worldward_thrust
- **def:** A has more than B when A's sentences terminate in actions already taken or irrevocably committed, while B's terminate in stances held; the pressure from words toward world.
- **smell:** Requires distinguishing reports of deeds from deed-flavored positioning — a world-model of what actually happened versus what is being performed as happening.
- **top:** A launch post whose artifact already runs, written as a by-product of the deed rather than a substitute for it.
- **confounder:** Imperative-verb and "we shipped" phrase density.

## irreversibility_appetite
- **def:** A has more than B when A closes more of its own author's exits — dated falsifiable predictions, shipping under one's own name, frozen APIs, burned bridges — where B keeps every retreat open.
- **smell:** Judging what genuinely forecloses options demands counterfactual modeling of the author's future: what could they no longer say or do after publishing this.
- **top:** A dated, quantified public prediction staked on the author's professional standing, published where it cannot be quietly edited.
- **confounder:** Bold, confident tone.

## progenitive_surplus
- **def:** A has more than B when A leaks more *viable* starting points it doesn't itself pursue — asides, footnotes, and tools-built-in-passing that could each sustain someone else's project.
- **smell:** Requires judging the viability of throwaway fragments, which takes domain-deep latent knowledge; counting digressions is worthless because most digressions are dead.
- **top:** A Feynman-lecture-grade piece whose footnotes have spawned literatures.
- **confounder:** Digression count and idea-list length.

## voltage_off_camera
- **def:** A has more than B when A burns hottest exactly where no audience reward exists — error paths, appendices, edge-case enumerations, commit messages — while B's care tracks the spotlight.
- **smell:** The judge needs a map of where audience attention lands for the genre, to detect care spent in its dead zones; polish-detection collapses this entirely.
- **top:** A repo whose error messages and test harness are more ferocious than its README.
- **confounder:** Overall polish.

## momentum_not_motion
- **def:** A has more than B when A's activity compounds — each move consumes the retained output of prior moves — while B's equal volume of activity oscillates and churns.
- **smell:** Requires reconstructing the dependency graph among the artifact's moves: did step N build on step N−1's result or merely follow it chronologically.
- **top:** A multi-year devlog where version nine visibly stands on decisions locked in version one.
- **confounder:** Activity volume and update frequency.

## friction_metabolized
- **def:** A has more than B when A's surface implies more swallowed drudgery — data cleaned, formats fought, edge cases enumerated — digested silently rather than narrated for credit.
- **smell:** The invisible work must be inferred from its residue, which requires knowing what each result actually costs to produce in that domain.
- **top:** A dataset paper whose forty-page appendix of collection anomalies simply exists, unremarked.
- **confounder:** Explicit effort narration ("this took six months of pain").

## live_computation
- **def:** A has more than B when A shows evidence its content was computed during writing — a claim revised by its own paragraph-later consequence, genuine surprise at its own result — while B replays cached conclusions.
- **smell:** Separating on-page inference from rehearsed discovery theater requires modeling what the author could have known before writing began.
- **top:** A Feynman problem-session transcript where the author takes a wrong turn and eats it publicly.
- **confounder:** Informal first-person tone and "wait, actually" tics.

## conscriptive_gravity
- **def:** A has more than B when A leaves a competent reader with reallocated priorities — plans bent around the artifact — where B leaves the reader with mere approval.
- **smell:** Requires simulating a capable reader's planning process and measuring the diff it undergoes; engagement metrics don't touch this because agreement and conscription are different forces.
- **top:** A manifesto-with-working-code that made a generation of engineers change what they were building — the Linux announcement email.
- **confounder:** Call-to-action language and follower counts.

## stake_density
- **def:** A has more than B when being wrong costs A's author more per sentence — reputation, capital, years of life already wagered — independent of how confident either sounds.
- **smell:** Needs a model of the author's actual exposure, social and financial and professional, inferable only from deep contextual knowledge of who says this and what happens to them if it's false.
- **top:** A scientist or investor publishing the full reasoning behind a bet already placed with their own career or capital.
- **confounder:** Confidence of tone; absence of hedging words.

## counterpunch_exposure
- **def:** A has more than B when more of A's content could be destroyed by a specific observable outcome — falsifiable claims, running code, dated forecasts, reproducible numbers — while B occupies terrain reality cannot reach.
- **smell:** Requires deriving, per claim, what concrete future evidence would kill it; mapping claims to their potential refuters is expensive inference, not sentiment.
- **top:** A benchmark-stakes systems claim shipped with its own reproduction script.
- **confounder:** Combative or contrarian tone.

## refusal_to_stay_dead
- **def:** A has more than B when A carries visible strata of prior failed attempts at the same problem that it out-survived — resurrections evidenced in the structure, not the self-narration.
- **smell:** Distinguishing genuine attempt-shaped geology from grit memoir requires reading the artifact's strata: what design decisions only make sense as scar tissue from a specific earlier death.
- **top:** The library that is its author's fourth public attempt at one problem, each prior corpse cited.
- **confounder:** Struggle-narrative phrases ("I almost gave up").

## resolution_throughput
- **def:** A has more than B when A converts more of the questions it opens into settled positions with mechanism, while B curates its open questions as ambiance.
- **smell:** Requires keeping an open/closed ledger for every question raised across the whole artifact and verifying each closure has actual mechanism behind it — whole-argument bookkeeping plus judgment of whether the closure is real.
- **top:** A design doc that raises eleven objections and kills each one with a decision.
- **confounder:** Assertion density; listicle-style decisiveness.

## reach_with_grip
- **def:** A has more than B when A's grandest claim stands closer, in unbroken mechanism, to something A has actually built or derived — the cathedral and the trowel in one artifact.
- **smell:** Grandiosity and concreteness are each trivially fakeable alone; measuring the mechanism-distance between summit claim and ground-truth work requires evaluating whether every intermediate rung is real.
- **top:** A cosmic-scope proposal whose section three is a working prototype.
- **confounder:** Grand vocabulary with a code block nearby.

## annealed_conviction
- **def:** A has more than B when A's certainty shows deformation marks from having absorbed the *strongest* counterargument, while B's equal certainty was never load-tested.
- **smell:** The judge must know the actual steelman for the position to tell whether the counterarguments engaged were the real ones or convenient sparring dummies.
- **top:** A position paper by an author who previously published the opposite view and shows the fracture line.
- **confounder:** "To be fair"/"steelmanning this" boilerplate; hedge-then-assert structure.

## sealed_box_intolerance
- **def:** A has more than B when A descends further down the stack than its task required — deriving the math, reading the source, rebuilding the tool — refusing to rest on any abstraction the author hasn't personally opened.
- **smell:** Knowing which boxes were actually opened versus name-dropped requires the judge to know what's inside the boxes.
- **top:** A bug writeup that descends from JavaScript through the runtime into a CPU erratum because the author would not accept "flaky."
- **confounder:** Jargon depth and citation count.

## recovery_torque
- **def:** A has more than B when setbacks appearing mid-artifact — an error found, a result invalidated — are converted into forward rotation that *uses the hit's information content*, rather than hidden or absorbed as narrated damage.
- **smell:** Requires assessing whether the post-hit direction actually exploits what the failure revealed — turning into the skid versus performing resilience — which means understanding what the failure taught.
- **top:** A lab notebook where the fatal flaw discovered in week two becomes the paper's actual contribution.
- **confounder:** Pivot-story frequency.
