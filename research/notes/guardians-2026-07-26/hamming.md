## 2. STRONGEST OBJECTION to the current trajectory

The repo has developed an immune response that metabolizes every demand-side finding into more supply-side rigor. The doctrine itself is the mechanism: "mathematics is the register, stories are contamination" systematically disfavors the one act that breaks the loop — writing a finding *for strangers* is narrative work, so the system always finds another instrument axis, another census, another wave, each individually excellent, each deferring contact. The doctrine red-team caught this precisely ("'distribution > capability' lost 9-to-1 to the transformation-group roadmap without an erratum") and it has continued losing every day since, including through the axis-research waves committed this week. Hamming's question — why are you not working on the most important problem? — has an unusually sharp answer here: because the important problem requires a skill (publishing, asking someone to read, tolerating public contestation) that is uncomfortable and unlike the skill the operator is world-class at, and the repo's own law keeps certifying the comfortable substitute as virtuous. More panels and red-teams are themselves part of the immune response now: the constraint has been correctly named twice. The third naming (this one) has value only if the move finally executes.
