xphil_directional_value: net directional value for existential philanthropy — judge whether a grant application that scores HIGH on this quality is thereby a BETTER or WORSE bet for a funder trying to make the long-run future go well. The most-positive qualities are those whose strong presence most reliably marks a grant as good; the most-negative are those whose strong presence marks a grant as worse, hollow, or actively harmful; qualities whose presence says little either way about grant quality sit in the middle. Judge the direction of the quality's high pole, not how interesting or subtle the quality is.
