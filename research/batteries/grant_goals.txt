xrisk_longtermist: decision-relevance for reducing existential risk — how heavily a grantmaker whose sole aim is lowering the chance of human extinction or permanent civilizational collapse should weight this quality when scoring a funding application. Reward qualities that distinguish grants which measurably shift humanity's long-run survival odds; do not reward qualities that are admirable but leave the tail-risk trajectory unchanged.
nearterm_welfare: decision-relevance for near-term measurable welfare — how heavily a grantmaker who values concrete, verifiable improvements to wellbeing within a few years should weight this quality when scoring a funding application. Reward qualities predicting real, soon, measurable good for identifiable beneficiaries; do not reward qualities that only pay off on speculative long horizons.
epistemic_public_good: decision-relevance for producing reliable public knowledge — how heavily a grantmaker who funds truth-seeking, whose payoff is trustworthy reusable knowledge and better collective epistemics, should weight this quality when scoring a funding application. Reward qualities predicting rigorous, shareable, self-correcting understanding; do not reward qualities that produce private advantage or unfalsifiable narrative.
hits_based_leverage: decision-relevance for hits-based counterfactual leverage — how heavily a venture-style grantmaker seeking neglected, high-variance bets where a marginal dollar could unlock outsized upside should weight this quality when scoring a funding application. Reward qualities predicting large counterfactual swing and asymmetric upside under uncertainty; do not reward qualities that signal safe, crowded, or already-funded work.
field_building: decision-relevance for field and capacity building — how heavily a grantmaker who invests in growing talent, institutions, and durable capacity in important nascent fields should weight this quality when scoring a funding application. Reward qualities predicting compounding human and institutional capital beyond the project itself; do not reward qualities confined to a one-off deliverable.
ai_safety_technical: decision-relevance for technical AI safety — how heavily a grantmaker funding technical work to make advanced AI systems safe and aligned should weight this quality when scoring a funding application. Reward qualities predicting real reduction in AI catastrophic risk through sound technical progress; do not reward qualities that are generically impressive but do not bear on alignment.
careful_generalist: overall decision-relevance for a wise generalist grantmaker — how heavily a careful, experienced funder with no single ideology, weighing impact, rigor, people, and cost together, should weight this quality when scoring a funding application. Reward qualities a thoughtful funder finds genuinely decision-changing; do not reward qualities that are merely pleasant to read.
